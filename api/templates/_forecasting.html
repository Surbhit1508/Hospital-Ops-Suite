Finding: seasonal-naive (repeat last year's pattern) performs far worse than even the plain naive baseline — COVID admission waves are epidemic-driven, not calendar-periodic, so assuming fixed yearly seasonality actively hurts here. A damped-trend Holt-Winters model, which adapts locally instead of assuming a fixed season, wins and beats naive with p < 0.001.
