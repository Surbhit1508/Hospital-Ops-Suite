Finding: this used to show a bed shortfall (Emergency vs. Surgery) — it turned out to be caused by a biased upstream NLP classifier over-predicting 'Surgery' for 44% of real notes, not a real capacity problem. Fixing the NLP model's class imbalance (see NLP section above) rebalanced the case mix fed into this LP, and the shortage disappeared entirely: every department is now fully served within real capacity (ICU usage ~87%, total beds ~63%). The reasoning that produced the old finding was correct given its inputs — the inputs themselves were wrong. A pipeline where one model's output feeds another model's decision can silently manufacture a downstream problem that looks completely legitimate until you trace it back upstream.
